# RA-L Controlled Keyword Vocabulary

**Source:** RA-L (IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters) keyword list as shown in PaperPlaza, supplied by the user on 2026-11-07. RAS revises it, so re-check it in PaperPlaza before submitting.

**When to use:** whenever the target venue is RA-L, including RA-L papers drafted as `IEEE_CONF`. ICRA / IROS use [`icra-keywords.md`](icra-keywords.md) instead; the two lists differ (see "Differences from the ICRA list" below).

**RA-L keyword rules:**

1. **2–5 keywords**, placed just beneath the abstract.
2. **Keywords 1 and 2 are copied verbatim from this list** and are the ones selected in PaperPlaza. The `\begin{IEEEkeywords}` block starts with the same two strings, in the same order.
3. **Keywords 3–5 are optional.** Prefer terms from this list; use a free-form term only for a concept central to the paper that the list lacks (e.g. `Diffusion Policy`).
4. Subject areas carry no priority tiers. The ICRA 2027 one-per-tier rule and its 250-character limit belong to ICRA.

**Selecting:** keyword 1 names the primary contribution area; keyword 2 names the method or a second area, ideally from a different subject area. The archetypes and anti-patterns at the bottom of [`icra-keywords.md`](icra-keywords.md) carry over: every term they recommend is also on this list. Read them with the tier constraint removed.

## Differences from the ICRA list

Compared with [`icra-keywords.md`](icra-keywords.md) (2026 cycle):

- **Only on the ICRA list (unavailable for RA-L):** `Robotics in Under-Resourced Settings`, `Competition`.
- **Only on the RA-L list:** `AI and Machine Learning in Manufacturing and Logistics Systems`, `Computer Integrated Manufacturing`, `Cyber-physical Production Systems and Industry 4.0`, `Data Analytics for Manufacturing and Logistics Systems`, `Materials Handling`, `Production Management`, `Production Planning and Scheduling`, `Productivity`.
- **`Imitation Learning` is a first-class choice.** On the ICRA list it sits in tier 4, outside the 3-tier rule; RA-L has no tiers.
- **Same term, different subject area.** RA-L files the three `Aerial Systems: …` terms under *Aerial and Field Robotics*, `Distributed Robot Systems` / `Swarm Robotics` under *Multiple and Distributed Systems*, `Deep Learning in Grasping and Manipulation` / `Mobile Manipulation` / `Perception for Grasping and Manipulation` under *Autonomy for Mobility and Manipulation*, and `Physical Human-Robot Interaction` under *Human-Robot Interaction*. ICRA's *Logistics* area is *Automation and Logistics* here, and also lists `Additive Manufacturing` / `Semiconductor Manufacturing`. Report the RA-L subject area, not the ICRA one.
